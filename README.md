# Lightchain AI DUNA — Public Documents

This folder contains documents and resources made publicly available by the Lightchain AI DUNA.

## Purpose

These materials are provided to support transparency, community participation, and access to information about DUNA activities and governance.

## Document Status

Each document should be reviewed according to its stated status:

- **Draft** — Under review and subject to change
- **Proposed** — Submitted for consideration or approval
- **Approved** — Formally approved through the applicable process
- **Executed** — Finalized and signed, where signatures are required
- **Archived** — Retained for historical reference

Unless expressly marked **Approved** or **Executed**, a document should not be considered final or binding.

## Important Notice

Public availability does not grant permission to modify, republish, or commercially use these materials unless a document or accompanying license expressly permits it.

If a document conflicts with an executed agreement or an official governance record, the executed agreement or official governance record controls.

## Corrections and Questions

Questions, corrections, or concerns may be submitted through this repository’s Issues section.
